About Party Cat

What is Party Cat?

Party Cat is a free, open-source party planning app, built by me, Maeve Lander.

I am not, in fact, a party animal myself. But I do love a good list to help ward off party cat-astrophies! I built this kitten-kaboodle to make organising my kids' birthday parties fun and easy. I hope Party Cat makes you smile, and helps make your own party plans purrrfect.

Who is it for?

Anyone planning a party who'd rather keep the guest list, the jobs and the shopping in one place than spread across three apps and a note on the fridge. I use it for my kids' birthdays, and it works just as well for barbecues, baby showers and dinner parties.

It's free, there's nothing to install, and you don't need an account. Start planning your party.

Your Data & Open Source

No account: Your party stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account: Your party is saved on Party Cat's server so you can open it on any device, and only you can see it.

Privacy: No ads, no tracking cookies and no data selling. I only keep basic anonymous stats, like how many people use Party Cat.

Open source: Anyone can view, use or improve the code on GitHub.
