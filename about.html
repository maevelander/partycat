About Party Cat

What is Party Cat?

Party Cat is a free, open-source party planner. It keeps your RSVPs, to-do list and shopping list in one place, with a live headcount of who's coming.

Type "Sam & Alex" or "The Smiths family" and it counts the people for you. Pick from four themes with the cat button in the corner.

Your Data

No account: Your party stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account: Your party is saved on this site's server so you can open it on any device, and only you can see it.

See Legal for the details.

Open Source

Party Cat was created by Maeve Lander. Anyone can view, use or improve the code on GitHub, where contributions and bug reports are welcome.
