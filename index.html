Questions

For the curious cats.

Is Party Cat free?

Yes. Party Cat is completely free, with no ads, paid tiers or limits on guests.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can plan a whole party without signing up. An account is optional, and lets you open your party on more than one device.

Where is my party data stored?

Without an account, everything stays in your own browser. With an account, your party is saved to Party Cat's server and only you can see it. I never sell data or use tracking cookies.

How does Party Cat count guests?

It reads the names you type. "Sam & Alex" counts as 2 people, "Mateo, Ben, Ava" as 3, and "The Smiths family" as 4, so your headcount is ready for catering.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Party Cat works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to install.

Who made Party Cat?

I'm Maeve Lander. I'm more list-lover than party animal, and I made this kitten-kaboodle for my kids' parties. It's open source on GitHub.
