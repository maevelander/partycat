What is Party Cat?

Party Cat is a free, open-source party planning app created by Maeve Lander. It's designed to help you organise your guest list and party tasks with a smile.

Privacy & Data Processing

Guest Mode: Your data never leaves your device - it's stored locally in your browser.

Account Mode: If you create an account, your email address and the data you enter into the app are stored securely in a database. Your email is only used to log you in and to reset your password.

You can delete the data you enter into the app at any time - simply tap "Clear Data".

I keep basic usage stats, like how many people use Party Cat and how often. No cookies, no advertising, no third-party tracking.
