What Party Cat Stores

Without an account: Everything you enter stays in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

With an account: Your email address, a hashed password and the party data you enter are stored in this site's database. Your email is only used to log you in and to reset your password.

Usage stats: The app keeps a random ID in your browser and records at most one anonymous visit per day, to count how many people use it. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party services.

You can delete the party data you've entered at any time with "Clear Data".
